FAQ

Quick answers

How do I get fibre installed?

Choose a package and send your name, phone number and location through the contact form or message us on WhatsApp. We will confirm availability and guide you through the next steps.

What payment methods do you accept?

We offer flexible payment methods for both fibre and hotspot services. Contact us for the options available for your chosen package.

How does Denop Hostel WiFi prepaid work?

Select a period (24 Hours, 1 Week or 1 Month), contact us, and we will guide you through payment and activation.

How do vouchers work?

If you already have a voucher code, use it to connect or contact us for help. If you need a voucher, request one via WhatsApp and we will assist you.

Are the fibre speeds guaranteed?

Plans are advertised as speeds up to the listed maximum. Actual performance can vary based on network conditions, equipment and usage.

Where do you operate?

Fibre coverage includes Kabalagala, Kansanga, Muyenga, Kiwafu, Luwafu, Kitalanga, Soya, Bunga, Kiwempe, Salaama Road, Munyonyo, Kibuli, Bukasa, Kizungu and surrounding areas. More neighbourhoods are being added. Contact us to confirm your location.

Ready to get online?

Fibre installation or hotspot access — message us and we will help you choose the right option.
