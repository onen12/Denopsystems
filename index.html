SUPPORT

Quick answers.

How do I get fibre installed?

Send your name, phone and location on WhatsApp or use the form below. We confirm coverage, agree your plan, then schedule your installation.

Is my area covered?

We cover 14+ areas in south Kampala and are growing. Use “Check my address” and we’ll confirm on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

We offer flexible options for fibre and hotspot. Ask us on WhatsApp for the current payment details.

Are the speeds guaranteed?

Speeds are “up to” the plan maximum and can vary with network conditions, distance and your equipment.

How do hotspot vouchers work?

Buy a package or voucher, connect to Denop Hostel WiFi and enter your code. Need help? Message us.

Can I get fibre for my business?

Yes. Our Challenger plan is built for small businesses. Tell us your needs and we’ll advise.
